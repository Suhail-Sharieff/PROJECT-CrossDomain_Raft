Problems
Paxos was overly complicated and hard to understand, hence it was hard to debug
Raft was introduced, but due to constraints in speed of light in optical fibers, deployment in WAN became hard
Raft was deployed, but there was high latency max around 200+ ms
In Raft, a single leader used to send ACK to all followers, hence complex management
Client ---Request---> Leader
Leader ---AppendEntries---> all followers
Followers ---->ACK----> Leader
Leader Commits ----CommitEntry---->Followers
----------------------------------------------------------
Solution: CD Raft
uses Tpopology aware heirarchial concensus
intra domain latency and interdomain latency is reduced by half
--------------------------------------------------------
Features:
decouples inter and intra domain replication
parallel concensus happens in inter and intra domain, rather than one single concensus as in raft
Fast return strategy to enable local domani leader to directly respond local client instead of waiting for global leader to respond as in raft
Reduction of load on a single node and better load balancing

Results:
50% write latency reduction in WAN compared to raft in local and global level
95% read latency reduction at global and 97% in local domain level
3X throughput/ operations per sec compared to raft
MTTR(mean time to recover) is almost 2ms which is 300X faster than  standard raft

75% reduction in cross domain egress volume traffic
